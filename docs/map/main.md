# Map: `addons/main`

Part of [UPSTREAM-MAP.md](../UPSTREAM-MAP.md). Line numbers refer to the tree at the time of
writing (commit `b1885db`).

`addons/main` is the shared foundation the other LAMBS components build on. `config.cpp` pulls
in `CfgEventHandlers.hpp`, which registers three XEH hooks: `XEH_preStart.sqf` (compiles the
function list from `XEH_PREP.hpp`), `XEH_preInit.sqf` (compiles again, loads the CBA settings
from `settings.inc.sqf`, creates the share-handler list and the suppression-blocking model
cache, and on clients spawns a version-mismatch check), and `XEH_postInit.sqf` (fills the
model cache from `CfgVehicles`, sets up the debug colour table, and registers CBA events
`lambs_main_OnCheckBody/OnPanic/OnInformationShared/OnFleeing` plus deprecated
`lambs_danger_*` mirrors and a `doSwitchMove` global event). `script_macros.hpp` adds AI
ammo-usage flag constants and the `GET_CURATOR_*_UNDER_CURSOR` Zeus macros. The 86 functions
under `functions/` are the shared helpers: knowledge sharing, stress, the tactical position
system, cover and formation movement primitives, unit and vehicle actions, static-weapon
drills, Zeus dialog plumbing and debug drawing. Nothing in `main` runs a scheduler of its own;
every cycle is either called by the `danger` brain and tactics, re-arms itself with
`CBA_fnc_waitAndExecute`, or is a `CBA_fnc_waitUntilAndExecute` poll with a timeout.

Cost legend: **trivial** (no world queries), **cheap** (one bounded world query or ray),
**moderate** (several queries or rays, or an all-groups or all-units filter), **expensive**
(repeated place searches or unbounded scans). Cadence: per-frame, per-tick (repeating cycle),
per-event (called on a trigger), one-shot.

- `settings.inc.sqf:248`: `addMissionEventHandler ["Draw3D", { call FUNC(debugDraw); }]` when the `debug_Drawing` setting is switched on (removed at line 252 when switched off).
